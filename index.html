Our Story

Made with Passion

Annapoorani Hotel & Catering is a beloved dining destination nestled in the bustling Tatabad neighborhood of Gandhipuram, Coimbatore. Named after the Goddess of Food and Nourishment, Annapoorani lives up to its name by serving wholesome, flavorful, and freshly prepared South Indian meals every single day. From steaming idlis at breakfast to hearty rice meals at lunch and dinner, we bring the warmth of traditional Tamil cuisine to your table.

With our doors open from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, seven days a week, we are here whenever hunger calls. Beyond our restaurant, we offer professional catering services for events, functions, and celebrations across Coimbatore. Whether it is an intimate family gathering or a large corporate event, our experienced catering team ensures every guest is served with care, quality, and the unmistakable taste of home-cooked goodness.
